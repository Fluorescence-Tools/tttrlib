Bi-exponential model#

fit24#

Fit24 optimizes is a bi-exponential model function with two fluorescence lifetimes :math:` au_1`, :math:` au_2`, and amplitude of the second lifetime \(a_2\), the fraction scattered light \(\gamma\), and a constant offset to experimental data. Fit24 does not describe anisotropy. The decays passed to the fit in the Jordi format.
