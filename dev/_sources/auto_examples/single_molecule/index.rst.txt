Single-molecule
---------------

Application of ``tttrlib`` to single-molecule data.

The examples below cover burst search, FRET burst summaries, microtime
selection, intensity traces, and PDA workflows. Sphinx-Gallery generates the
example list automatically from the scripts in this directory.

Emissions are the caller&#x27;s business — a Gaussian mixture, a Poisson rate, a lookup table — which is exactly what lets one lattice serve every model.
